Payday Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Payday is a budgeting app built around your paycheck. This page explains what happens to your information.

The short version

  • You don't create an account. Payday doesn't ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • Your budget is stored on your phone and encrypted.
  • Payday doesn't connect to your bank.
  • Payday has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We don't sell your data.

What Payday stores

Everything you enter: your pay schedule and amounts, bills, purchases, savings goals, balances, and settings. It is saved on your phone only, in the app's private storage. It is encrypted with a key kept in your phone's secure storage (the iPhone Keychain or Android Keystore). The key stays on that phone.

What Payday does not collect

Payday does not collect your budget, your location, your contacts, or your device identifiers. The app does not send your budget to us or to anyone else.

Face ID and PIN

If you turn on app lock, your phone checks your face or passcode. Payday never receives your biometric data.

Notifications

Reminders (bill due, payday) are scheduled on your phone. They don't go through our servers.

Backups and exports

You can save a backup file or a CSV export from Settings. You choose where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, email, and so on). Once a file leaves the app, it's under that service's privacy policy. Backup files from your phone's own system backups (such as iCloud or Google backups) may include Payday's data, in encrypted form. The encryption key does not move to a new phone, so restore from a Payday backup file there.

Feedback

If you choose Send feedback, Payday prepares a message with what you typed, the impact you picked, and the app and system version. It doesn't include your budget. You can read and edit it before sending. If you send it by email, we see your email address and anything you wrote.

Payments

Payday Plus is sold through Apple's App Store (or Google Play). Apple or Google handles your payment and subscription. We don't see your card details.

Deleting your data

Settings, then Your data, then Erase everything removes your budget from the phone. Deleting the app does too. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Children

Payday isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. If Payday ever starts sending your data off your phone, we'll say so here and in the app before it happens.
